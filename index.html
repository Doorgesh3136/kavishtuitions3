How it works

Real students. Trusted partners. Your money, where it matters.

Your contribution goes directly to the university — and you get to watch it make a difference.

How it works for donors

Real students, verified by real institutions.

01Donors

Choose your monthly contribution

Pick an amount that works for you. Even a small recurring gift can change what’s possible for someone.

02Partners

Students are verified by trusted partners

Students already in a partner’s scholarship pipeline or admitted to their programs.

03ConneQt

Donors are matched with a student

You’re connected with a specific person whose education your contribution helps make possible.

04Partners & ConneQt

Funds go directly to the university

90% of your contribution is paid directly to the institution, not to an individual or intermediary. You can see exactly where it goes.

05Donors & Students

Stay connected

As your student moves forward, you move with them. Updates, milestones and real moments build a relationship over time.

Why ConneQt

ConneQt is the transparent platform connecting donors with verified students. You sponsor a real student, your money goes to their university, and you follow their journey.

90% of your contribution is paid directly to the student’s institution every month, not to an individual or an intermediary. You can see exactly where it goes.

Institutions decide who gets support, based on need and potential, not donors or the platform. You are then matched with a specific, verified student.

90% of every contribution goes directly to the institution to cover the student’s tuition.
